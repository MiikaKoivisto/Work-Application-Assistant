TITLE: Restore deleted files or folders in Microsoft OneDrive
PRODUCT: OneDrive
CATEGORY: File Recovery
SOURCE: Microsoft Support
SOURCE_URL: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/onedrive/restore-deleted-files-or-folders-in-onedrive

---

OVERVIEW

Files or folders deleted from OneDrive may be recoverable from the OneDrive recycle bin.

The recycle bin provides a way to restore content that was deleted accidentally.

RECYCLE BIN

Deleted OneDrive items can be placed in the recycle bin instead of immediately disappearing permanently.

Users can open the OneDrive recycle bin to look for files or folders they want to recover.

FIND A DELETED ITEM

The user should locate the required file or folder in the recycle bin.

The item can then be selected for recovery.

RESTORE AN ITEM

A deleted item can be restored from the recycle bin when it is still available there.

After restoration, the file or folder becomes available again in OneDrive.

RECOVERY LIMITATIONS

Deleted content is not necessarily retained indefinitely.

Retention behavior can depend on the type of account and the organization's Microsoft 365 configuration.

Users should therefore attempt to recover accidentally deleted files as soon as possible.

ITEM NOT AVAILABLE

If the required file is no longer available in the recycle bin, normal recycle-bin restoration may not be possible.

In a managed organization, users may need to contact their IT support team when important content cannot be recovered through the available OneDrive recovery options.
