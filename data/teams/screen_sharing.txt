OVERVIEW

Microsoft Teams allows users to share content with other participants.

Screen sharing is useful when another person needs to see an application, presentation, document or other content displayed on the user's computer.

SHARE A SCREEN

A user can start sharing from the Teams sharing controls.

The available sharing options depend on the Teams experience and the content available on the device.

ENTIRE SCREEN

Users can share an entire screen.

When an entire screen is shared, participants can see the content displayed on that screen while sharing is active.

This can be useful when the presenter needs to move between several applications.

SHARE A WINDOW

Users can choose to share a specific application window instead of the entire screen.

Sharing only a window can help prevent participants from seeing unrelated applications or other content on the desktop.

DURING SHARING

While sharing content, the presenter can continue communicating with the other participants.

The presenter should be aware of what information is visible when sharing an entire screen.

STOP SHARING

Screen sharing can be stopped when the presenter no longer wants other participants to see the shared content.

After sharing is stopped, participants no longer receive the presenter's shared screen or window.
