OVERVIEW

Microsoft Teams uses notifications to inform users about activity that may require their attention.

Users can adjust notification behavior to reduce unnecessary interruptions or make important activity easier to notice.

NOTIFICATION SETTINGS

Teams provides settings for controlling notifications.

Users can review notification preferences and decide how they want to be notified about different types of activity.

CHANNEL NOTIFICATIONS

Notifications can be managed for Teams channels.

A user may want notifications for important channel activity while reducing notifications from channels that are less relevant.

Different channels can therefore have different notification behavior.

REDUCING NOTIFICATIONS

Users who receive too many Teams notifications can change their notification preferences.

Reducing less important notifications can make it easier to notice messages and activity that require attention.

IMPORTANT ACTIVITY

Users should consider which Teams activity is important for their work before disabling notifications.

Notification settings can be adjusted so that important communication remains visible while less useful alerts are reduced.

MANAGING PREFERENCES

Notification preferences can be changed again later.

This allows users to adapt Teams notifications when their responsibilities, teams or communication needs change.
