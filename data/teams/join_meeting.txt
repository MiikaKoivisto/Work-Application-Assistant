OVERVIEW

Microsoft Teams provides several ways to join an online meeting.

The available method depends on how the meeting invitation was received and whether the user is using Teams.

JOIN FROM A CALENDAR

A scheduled meeting can be opened from the Teams calendar.

When the meeting is ready, the user can select the option to join.

The user may be shown a pre-join screen before entering the meeting.

JOIN FROM A MEETING LINK

Teams meeting invitations can contain a link for joining the meeting.

Opening the meeting link starts the process of connecting to the meeting.

Depending on the environment, the user may be able to join using the Teams application or another supported experience.

PRE-JOIN SETTINGS

Before entering a meeting, users can review available audio and video settings.

For example, a user may choose whether the camera or microphone should be enabled before joining.

Reviewing these settings can prevent unwanted audio or video from being transmitted when entering the meeting.

MEETING LOBBY

Some meetings use a lobby.

A participant may need to wait in the lobby until someone in the meeting allows them to enter.

Lobby behavior depends on the meeting settings configured by the organizer or organization.

TROUBLE JOINING

If a user cannot join a meeting, they should check that they are using the correct meeting invitation or link.

They should also verify their internet connection and the audio or video devices they intend to use.
