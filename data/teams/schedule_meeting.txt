OVERVIEW

Microsoft Teams allows users to schedule online meetings for a future date and time.

Scheduled meetings can include invited participants and meeting details.

TEAMS CALENDAR

A meeting can be created from the calendar in Microsoft Teams.

Users can select an available time or create a new calendar event and provide the meeting information.

MEETING DETAILS

The meeting can include information such as a title, date and time.

Additional details can be provided to explain the purpose of the meeting or give participants information they need before joining.

INVITE ATTENDEES

People can be invited when the meeting is created.

Invited participants receive information that allows them to join the scheduled meeting.

CALENDAR INTEGRATION

Scheduled Teams meetings can also appear in the user's Outlook calendar when the Microsoft 365 environment provides calendar integration.

This helps users manage their meetings through their normal calendar workflow.

CHANGE A MEETING

A scheduled meeting can be edited later.

For example, the meeting time or other details can be changed when plans change.

Updates can be sent to participants so they receive the revised meeting information.
